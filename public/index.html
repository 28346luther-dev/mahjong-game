      HOW TO RACE

      Join a scheduled multiplayer race for 2-minute qualifying and a 10-lap sprint with a mandatory 10-second pit stop. Free Practice still offers unlimited laps. Apex kerbs are red and white, with bright green paint and gravel runoff on the outside. Cross the chequered finish line to log each lap and immediately continue into the next. Engage the 40 km/h limiter with P before PIT IN. Keep it on until you rejoin the circuit at PIT OUT; your timed lap restarts after pitting. The long right-hand corner is banked for speed, and your S1/S2/S3 splits appear in the sector display. Use W to accelerate, A/D to steer, S or Space to brake. R resets your kart, but you'll need to restart for a valid lap.
